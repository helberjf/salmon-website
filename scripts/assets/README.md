# Fonte dos cartões sociais

`social-base-fjord.png` foi gerado como fundo editorial sem texto. O script
`../generate-social-cards.py` combina esse fundo com as fotografias aprovadas e
aplica toda a tipografia de forma determinística.

Prompt final usado para gerar o fundo:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: social sharing background for a premium
> B2B Norwegian salmon website. Primary request: Create a cinematic, refined wide
> background inspired by cold Norwegian fjords and the surface of deep ocean
> water, suitable for a luxury seafood trade brand connecting Norway and Brazil.
> Scene/backdrop: abstract-realistic coastal water and distant fjord silhouettes,
> no identifiable people, no product packaging. Style/medium: premium editorial
> photography with subtle modern brand-art direction. Composition/framing: very
> wide landscape, generous clean negative space on the left for later typography,
> visual interest and fjord/water detail concentrated on the right, safe crop for
> 1200x630. Lighting/mood: cool northern morning light, calm, confident,
> sophisticated. Color palette: deep navy #00383b, ocean teal #005357, pale frost
> #c1e4f2, very restrained coral #dd6c67 accent reflected in light only.
> Constraints: absolutely no text, no letters, no logos, no badges, no watermark,
> no flags, no boats; realistic salmon-industry-adjacent atmosphere without
> showing dead fish; high-end corporate look.
